# Resume Placeholder

Add your resume here as `resume.pdf`.

The "Download Resume" button in the hero section will download this file. Until you add it, the button will show an alert prompting you to add the file.

**Instructions:**
1. Name your resume file `resume.pdf`
2. Place it in this `public/` folder
3. The download button will automatically work

**Recommended:** Export your resume as PDF for best compatibility.
