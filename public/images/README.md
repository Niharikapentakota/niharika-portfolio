# Profile Photo Placeholder

Add your profile photo here as `profile.jpg`.

The site will automatically show a graceful fallback with your initials ("PN") until you add your real photo.

**Recommended:**
- Format: JPG or PNG
- Size: 500x500px or larger (square works best)
- File name: `profile.jpg`

After adding the file, refresh the site — your photo will appear in the hero section automatically.
